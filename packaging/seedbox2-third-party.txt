Seedbox 2 ships these programs next to its own code, unchanged and as separate executables in its bin folder:

ffmpeg 8.1.3 (https://ffmpeg.org), LGPL 2.1 or later. Built without GPL or non-free parts, with libopus (BSD) and LAME
  (LGPL 2 or later, https://lame.sourceforge.io), its MP3 encoder.
  Licence: https://www.gnu.org/licenses/old-licenses/lgpl-2.1.html
  Source: https://ffmpeg.org/releases/ffmpeg-8.1.3.tar.xz
  macOS: built by packaging/build_ffmpeg.sh in the Seedbox repository.
  Windows: the win64-lgpl build from https://github.com/BtbN/FFmpeg-Builds, whose release pages hold the build scripts.
  To use your own ffmpeg, replace that file, or point $SEEDBOX_BIN_DIR at a folder holding it.

deno (https://deno.com), MIT.

The Python packages inside the app keep their own licences, in the requirements of their distributions. Note
pedalboard (https://github.com/spotify/pedalboard), GPL 3.
